Privacy Policy

App: Yahrusalem Assemblies (com.yahrusalem.assemblies)
Last updated: 10 November 2026

Yahrusalem Assemblies is a Bible study and congregation app (lessons, Bible reader, calendar, games, announcements). This page explains what information the app uses and why.

Information we collect

  • Account details: the email address or login ID you sign up with, your password (stored securely by Firebase Authentication, we cannot read it), and an optional display name.
  • Sign-in and device information: a device description, last login time and a session token, used to keep your account working on one device and to prevent misuse.
  • Content you create in community features: announcements posted by leaders (title, text, audience, poster name), game and duel profiles and scores, and questions or posts you submit in features such as Youth Q&A.
  • Notification data: a push-notification token so the app can alert you (for example duel invitations and announcement approvals).
  • Location (optional): only when you tap "use my location" in the calendar, to calculate Sabbath times. It is used on your device for that calculation and is not stored on our servers.

Information kept only on your device

Your notes, bookmarks, reading progress, saved lessons, reminders, theme and language settings, and your age and gender entered for announcement audiences are stored on your phone only.

How we use it

To sign you in, show lessons and community content, run games and announcements, send notifications you have allowed, and keep the service secure. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

Services we use

The app stores account and community data with Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Cloud Messaging), operated by Google. Their handling of data is described at policies.google.com/privacy. Data is encrypted in transit.

Sharing

Community content you post (such as an announcement or game name) is visible to the audience it is posted for. We do not share your personal information with third parties except the service providers above, or where the law requires it.

Retention and deletion

Account data is kept while your account is active. To have your account and associated data deleted, email yahrusalemassemblies@gmail.com from your registered email, and we will delete it. You can remove on-device data at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.

Children

The app is intended for general audiences and members of the congregation. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without a parent's or guardian's involvement. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
